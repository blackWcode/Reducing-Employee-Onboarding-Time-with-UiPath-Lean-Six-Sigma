01 · Context

A growing company, a manual process

FuturaCore Ltd. is a fictional mid-sized tech company with 600+ employees across Dublin, Cork and Limerick. HR runs onboarding by hand, with emails, Excel trackers and shared folders, and each onboarding takes about 1 hour 30 minutes per employee.

  • Inconsistent task tracking across offices and officers
  • Frequent delays and data entry errors
  • No central visibility of where each new hire stands

FuturaCore and its dataset are simulated for this portfolio project. The figures below are from that dataset; the improvements are targets, not measured results.

02 · Method

Five phases of DMAIC

Select a phase to see what was done.

Define the problem and the goal

The manual process takes over 1h30 per hire, with frequent delays, errors and no central tracking. The business case sets four targets: halve onboarding time, automate at least half the manual tasks, cut errors, and give HR and leadership live dashboards.

See the targets ↓

Measure the current process

A SIPOC maps who supplies what, the process steps, the outputs and who receives them. 600 onboarding cases were recorded across 8 steps, with status, delay reason and processing time for each.

See the SIPOC ↓

Analyze where time is lost

Python (pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn) was used to clean the data, chart the processing-time distribution, compare offices, departments and HR officers, and check whether delays in different steps are related. A fishbone diagram groups the root causes.

Explore the analysis ↓

Improve with automation

UiPath is used to automate the repetitive steps (emails, folder creation and Excel updates), documented in a Process Design Document with current and future flows, inputs, outputs, exceptions and business rules.

See the improvements ↓

Control so the gains last

A control plan sets who checks what and how often, from daily automated checklists to quarterly SOP reviews, with a Power BI dashboard tracking average time, delays and errors.

See the control plan ↓

05 · Root causes

Fishbone: why onboarding is slow

Materials

  • Duplicate documents requested
  • Missing or incomplete forms
  • High dependency on manual follow-ups

People

  • Lack of standardised training
  • Limited tech awareness among HR staff
  • Unclear task ownership
Slow, error-prone onboarding

Process

  • No central checklist or SOP
  • Redundant steps across locations
  • Delays waiting on document approvals

Technology

  • No automation tools used initially
  • Manual Excel entries with frequent errors
